Renovation & Expansion Capabilities

Our Renew & Expand service addresses diverse renovation needs with the same quality standards we apply to new ADU construction. We handle structural modifications, electrical and plumbing upgrades, interior finishing, and exterior improvements. Our team excels at matching new construction to existing architectural styles, ensuring additions blend seamlessly with original structures. We also specialize in bringing older properties up to current building codes—an essential step for many renovation and conversion projects.

Beyond physical construction, we guide homeowners through the complex process of permits and approvals for renovation work. Many expansion projects require engineering analysis, architectural drawings, and multiple inspections. Our experience navigating Dallas building requirements streamlines approval processes and prevents costly delays. We also help clients understand how renovations affect property assessments and can advise on improvements that deliver the best return on investment.
