Upgrade Your Existing ADU in Dallas

Already have an ADU that needs updating? ADU Construction Dallas specializes in renovating and expanding existing accessory dwelling units throughout the Dallas-Fort Worth area. Whether your ADU is outdated, needs repairs, or you want to add more living space, our team delivers quality renovations that increase both comfort and value.

Many Dallas homeowners have older ADUs, converted spaces, or guest houses that could benefit from modern upgrades. Our renovation services breathe new life into these spaces, making them more functional, energy-efficient, and valuable as rental properties or family living spaces.

Our ADU Renovation Services

  • Kitchen Remodels: Modern cabinetry, countertops, appliances, and fixtures.
  • Bathroom Upgrades: New vanities, showers, tiles, and accessibility features.
  • Flooring Replacement: Hardwood, luxury vinyl, tile, or carpet installation.
  • HVAC Upgrades: Efficient heating and cooling systems for Dallas climate.
  • Electrical Updates: Modern wiring, panels, outlets, and smart home integration.
  • Plumbing Repairs: Pipe replacement, fixture upgrades, and water heater installation.
  • Insulation: Improve energy efficiency with modern insulation.
  • Windows & Doors: Energy-efficient replacements and security upgrades.

ADU Expansion Services

Need more space? We can expand your existing ADU within Dallas zoning limits:

  • Room Additions: Add a bedroom, bathroom, or living space.
  • Second-Story Additions: Build up when lot space is limited.
  • Bump-Outs: Extend walls for more interior square footage.
  • Patio Enclosures: Convert covered patios into conditioned living space.

When to Consider ADU Renovation

  • Your ADU is more than 10-15 years old
  • You're experiencing plumbing or electrical issues
  • Energy bills are higher than expected
  • You want to increase rental income potential
  • The layout doesn't meet current needs
  • You're preparing the property for sale
  • ADA accessibility modifications are needed
