ADU Construction Dallas is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We strive to provide an inclusive digital experience that allows all visitors to access the information and services we offer.

Our Commitment

We are dedicated to making our website conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. These guidelines are developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) and are widely regarded as the international standard for web accessibility.

Accessibility Features

Our website includes the following accessibility features:

Navigation & Structure

  • Consistent and predictable navigation across all pages
  • Descriptive page titles and headings for easy navigation
  • Breadcrumb navigation for clear page hierarchy
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and interactive elements
  • Skip-to-content links for screen reader users

Visual Design

  • Sufficient color contrast ratios between text and background
  • Resizable text without loss of functionality
  • No content that flashes more than three times per second
  • Responsive design that adapts to different screen sizes and devices

Images & Media

  • Alternative text (alt text) for all meaningful images
  • Decorative images properly marked to be ignored by screen readers
  • Video content with descriptive titles

Forms & Interactive Elements

  • Clearly labeled form fields with descriptive labels
  • Error messages that clearly identify and describe the issue
  • Sufficient time provided for form completion
  • Focus indicators for keyboard navigation

Assistive Technology Compatibility

Our website is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies:

  • Screen readers (JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver)
  • Screen magnification software
  • Speech recognition software
  • Keyboard-only navigation
  • Browser accessibility features and extensions

Browser Compatibility

For the best accessible experience, we recommend using the latest versions of:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Ongoing Efforts

We are continuously working to improve the accessibility of our website. Our efforts include:

  • Regular accessibility audits and testing
  • Training our team on accessibility best practices
  • Incorporating accessibility into our design and development process
  • Monitoring and addressing accessibility feedback from users
  • Updating our website to meet evolving accessibility standards

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some areas may still be in the process of improvement:

  • Some older PDF documents may not be fully accessible
  • Third-party content or plugins may have varying levels of accessibility
  • Some interactive maps may have limited screen reader support

We are actively working to address these limitations and improve the overall accessibility of our site.
