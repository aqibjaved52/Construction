Build Your Dream Backyard ADU in Dallas

A detached backyard ADU offers the ultimate in flexibility and privacy. ADU Construction Dallas specializes in building beautiful, custom accessory dwelling units in backyards throughout the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. Whether you envision a modern studio, a cozy cottage, or a spacious two-bedroom home, we bring your vision to life.

Backyard ADUs provide complete separation from your main home, making them ideal for long-term rentals, aging parents, adult children, or premium Airbnb listings. With higher rental potential than attached or converted units, a detached ADU is a smart investment in Dallas's growing housing market.

Benefits of a Detached Backyard ADU

  • Maximum Privacy: Separate entrance and complete independence from the main house.
  • Higher Rental Income: Detached units command premium rent in Dallas - typically $1,500-$2,500/month.
  • Design Flexibility: Build exactly what you want without structural limitations.
  • Property Value: Adds 20-35% to your property value.
  • Multi-Generational Living: Perfect for family members who need their own space.

Our Backyard Build Process

We provide comprehensive backyard ADU construction services from concept to completion:

  • Site Evaluation: Assess your backyard space, setbacks, utilities, and access points.
  • Custom Design: Create a floor plan optimized for your lot and lifestyle needs.
  • Permitting: Navigate Dallas zoning and building permits on your behalf.
  • Foundation: Pour concrete slab or pier-and-beam foundation as appropriate.
  • Framing & Structure: Build with quality lumber and modern construction techniques.
  • Utilities: Connect electrical, plumbing, gas, and HVAC systems.
  • Interior Finishes: Complete with drywall, flooring, cabinets, and fixtures.
  • Landscaping: Restore your yard and add finishing touches.

Backyard ADU Size Options

Dallas allows ADUs up to 850-1,000 square feet depending on your lot size and zoning. We offer:

  • Studio (350-450 sq ft): Perfect for home office, rental, or guest quarters.
  • One-Bedroom (500-650 sq ft): Full living space with separate bedroom.
  • Two-Bedroom (700-1,000 sq ft): Complete home with multiple bedrooms.
