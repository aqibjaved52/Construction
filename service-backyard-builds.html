Backyard Builds by Adu Construction Dallas transforms unused outdoor space into valuable, functional living areas. Detached backyard ADUs—also known as guest houses, granny flats, or backyard cottages—offer maximum privacy and flexibility for homeowners and occupants alike. Our specialized backyard construction team handles every challenge unique to backyard builds, from navigating tight access for construction equipment to designing structures that complement your existing home and landscape while maximizing usable yard space.

A detached backyard ADU provides distinct advantages over attached or conversion options. Complete separation from the main house offers superior privacy and noise isolation, making backyard units ideal for rental income, aging parents, adult children, or home offices requiring focused work environments. Our designs optimize backyard placement to preserve outdoor living areas while creating attractive structures that enhance overall property aesthetics. We consider factors like natural light, views, privacy screening, and landscape integration to deliver backyard ADUs that feel like intentional additions rather than afterthoughts.

Why Choose a Backyard ADU

Backyard ADUs represent one of the best investments Dallas homeowners can make. These detached structures typically command premium rental rates due to their privacy and stand-alone nature—often 20-30% higher than attached units. For homeowners planning multi-generational living arrangements, backyard ADUs provide independence while keeping family close. The physical separation also makes backyard units excellent for home-based businesses, creative studios, or private guest quarters that maintain boundaries between work and home life.

Our Backyard Builds service addresses the unique considerations of detached construction. We coordinate utility connections—water, sewer, electrical, and gas—with minimal disruption to existing systems. Our construction methods protect established landscaping whenever possible, and we plan site access carefully to prevent damage to yards, fences, and neighboring properties. From foundation to finishing, our backyard-focused approach delivers beautiful, functional ADUs that maximize your property's potential.

  • Complete privacy and separation from main residence.
  • Designs that complement existing home architecture.
  • Landscape-conscious construction minimizing yard impact.
  • Premium rental potential with detached living space.

FAQ

Backyard ADU Questions Answered

Minimum requirements vary by lot size and zoning, but generally you need enough space for the ADU footprint plus required setbacks (typically 4-5 feet from property lines). A 400-square-foot ADU needs roughly 600-700 square feet of backyard space accounting for setbacks. Larger ADUs require proportionally more space. We assess your specific lot during our free consultation.

We take extensive precautions to protect landscaping, including designated access paths, protective ground covers, and tree preservation measures. Some temporary impact is unavoidable, but we minimize damage and can include landscape restoration in project plans. We discuss site access and protection strategies during planning to address any specific concerns.

Utilities typically connect from your main house or the street. Water, sewer, and electrical lines run underground from connection points to the ADU. We coordinate with utility companies for any required upgrades to your service capacity. The ADU can share meters with your main house or have separate meters for independent utility billing—useful for rental situations.

Limited access is common and manageable. We've built backyard ADUs with access as narrow as 36 inches. For very tight sites, we use specialized equipment, prefabricated components, and careful material staging. Costs may increase slightly for challenging access, but we've successfully completed projects in nearly every backyard configuration.

Height limits for detached ADUs in Dallas typically allow single-story construction up to 16-20 feet depending on zoning. Two-story ADUs may be possible in some areas with appropriate setbacks and design considerations. Height restrictions often relate to neighbor privacy concerns. We research your specific lot's regulations and can design within allowable parameters.
