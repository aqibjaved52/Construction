Transform Your Dallas Garage into Valuable Living Space

Garage conversions are the most cost-effective way to add an ADU to your Dallas property. At ADU Construction Dallas, we specialize in transforming underutilized garages into beautiful, fully functional living spaces that generate rental income or provide housing for family members.

Whether you have a single-car, two-car, or detached garage, our expert team can convert it into a studio apartment, one-bedroom suite, or multi-room living space. Garage conversions typically cost 30-40% less than new construction while providing the same quality living experience.

Why Choose Garage Conversion?

  • Lower Cost: Starting at $80,000-$150,000, garage conversions are more affordable than new construction.
  • Faster Timeline: Most conversions are completed in 8-12 weeks.
  • Existing Structure: Foundation and walls are already in place, reducing construction complexity.
  • Easier Permits: Converting existing space often has a smoother permit process.
  • Immediate ROI: Start generating rental income quickly - Dallas rents for garage ADUs average $1,200-$1,800/month.

Our Garage Conversion Process

We handle every aspect of your garage conversion from start to finish:

  • Assessment: Evaluate your garage's structural condition, size, and conversion potential.
  • Design: Create floor plans maximizing your space with kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, and living areas.
  • Permits: Handle all Dallas permit applications and inspections.
  • Foundation Work: Upgrade flooring and ensure proper foundation for living space.
  • Utilities: Install plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and gas lines as needed.
  • Insulation & Drywall: Properly insulate walls and ceiling for Texas climate.
  • Finishes: Complete with flooring, fixtures, appliances, and final touches.
