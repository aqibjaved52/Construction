Benefits of Garage Conversion ADUs

Garage conversions offer unique advantages that make them an attractive option for Dallas homeowners. With lower construction costs compared to ground-up ADU builds, faster completion times, and minimal disruption to your property's landscape, converting your garage is a smart investment decision. Our team ensures that your converted space maintains proper ventilation, natural lighting, and all safety requirements while creating a comfortable living environment.

We understand that every garage presents different opportunities and challenges. Some conversions involve single-car garages suitable for studio apartments, while others transform spacious two-car garages into full one-bedroom units. Our design approach addresses common garage conversion considerations including ceiling height optimization, window placement for natural light, creating separate entrances, and ensuring adequate parking alternatives comply with local regulations.

Garage Conversion Questions Answered

Garage conversion costs in Dallas typically range from $50,000 to $150,000 depending on the garage size, desired finishes, and scope of work required. Single-car garage conversions start around $50,000-$80,000, while two-car garage conversions with premium finishes can reach $120,000-$150,000. We provide detailed, transparent estimates during your free consultation.

Yes, garage conversions in Dallas require building permits. Converting a garage to habitable living space involves significant changes to the structure that must be inspected for safety. We handle all permit applications, engineering requirements, and inspections as part of our full-service conversion package.

Dallas has relaxed parking requirements for ADUs in many areas. Depending on your property's location and proximity to public transit, you may be exempt from replacement parking requirements. Our team researches your specific zoning requirements and helps navigate any parking considerations during the planning phase.

Most garages can be converted, but feasibility depends on several factors including structural integrity, ceiling height, foundation condition, and property setbacks. During our free site assessment, we evaluate your garage's conversion potential and identify any structural improvements needed to meet building codes.

Once permits are approved, most garage conversions are completed within 8-12 weeks. The permit approval process typically takes 4-8 weeks. Total project timeline from signing to move-in is usually 3-5 months—significantly faster than new construction ADU builds which can take 6-12 months.
