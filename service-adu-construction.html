Comprehensive ADU Construction Services in Dallas

ADU Construction Dallas provides full-service accessory dwelling unit construction throughout the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. Our experienced team manages every phase of your ADU project, from initial planning and permitting to final finishes and inspection. We take the complexity out of ADU construction so you can focus on the exciting possibilities your new space will bring.

With over 500 ADUs completed across Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, Frisco, and surrounding areas, we have the expertise to handle projects of any size and complexity. Our turnkey approach means one team, one contract, and one point of contact throughout your entire project.

Our Full-Service Construction Includes

  • Site Preparation: Grading, excavation, and utility connection planning.
  • Foundation Work: Concrete slab, pier-and-beam, or raised foundation as needed.
  • Structural Framing: Quality lumber construction built to Texas codes.
  • Roofing: Durable roofing systems that match or complement your main home.
  • Plumbing: Complete plumbing installation including fixtures and water heater.
  • Electrical: Full electrical system with modern panel and smart-home ready wiring.
  • HVAC: Efficient heating and cooling systems sized for Dallas climate.
  • Insulation: High-performance insulation for energy efficiency.
  • Drywall & Paint: Professional finishing throughout.
  • Flooring: Your choice of hardwood, tile, vinyl, or carpet.
  • Cabinetry & Counters: Kitchen and bathroom installations.
  • Final Inspections: All required Dallas inspections and certificate of occupancy.

Construction Timeline

Typical ADU construction timelines in Dallas:

  • Garage Conversion: 8-12 weeks
  • Small Detached ADU (under 500 sq ft): 12-16 weeks
  • Large Detached ADU (500-1,000 sq ft): 16-20 weeks

Note: Permit approval adds 4-8 weeks before construction begins. We handle the entire permit process for you.
