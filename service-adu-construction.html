FAQ

ADU Construction Questions Answered

New construction ADU costs in Dallas typically range from $150,000 to $350,000 depending on size, finishes, and site conditions. A basic 400-square-foot studio starts around $150,000, while a premium 1,200-square-foot two-bedroom unit with high-end finishes can reach $350,000 or more. We provide detailed, itemized estimates during consultation.

New construction ADUs typically take 4-6 months to build once permits are approved. The permit process adds another 6-12 weeks. Total timeline from project start to completion is usually 6-9 months. We provide detailed construction schedules and keep you updated on progress throughout the build.

Yes, Adu Construction Dallas is fully licensed, bonded, and insured. We hold all required Texas contractor licenses and maintain comprehensive general liability and workers' compensation insurance. We're happy to provide proof of insurance and licensing upon request.

We provide a comprehensive warranty package including a 1-year workmanship warranty covering all construction work, plus manufacturer warranties on appliances, roofing, windows, and other materials. Structural elements carry a 10-year warranty. We also offer extended warranty options for additional peace of mind.

While we don't provide direct financing, we work with several lenders who specialize in ADU construction loans and can connect you with financing partners. Options include construction loans, home equity lines of credit (HELOC), and cash-out refinancing. We're happy to discuss payment structures that work with your financing timeline.
