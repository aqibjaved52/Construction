About James

James Mitchell founded ADU Construction Dallas in 2009 with a vision to help Dallas homeowners unlock the potential of their properties through quality accessory dwelling unit construction. With over two decades of experience in residential construction, James has led the company to become the most trusted ADU builder in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex.

Before founding ADU Construction Dallas, James spent 15 years working in custom home building and renovation, gaining valuable experience that he now applies to every ADU project. His hands-on approach and commitment to quality have earned the company an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau and hundreds of 5-star reviews from satisfied clients.

James is actively involved in advocating for ADU-friendly zoning policies in Dallas and regularly speaks at real estate and construction industry events about the benefits of accessory dwelling units for homeowners and communities.
